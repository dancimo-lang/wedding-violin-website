1. Services Provided
Daniel Cimo and his team ("Musician") agree to provide the following services for the Client's wedding:
Ceremony Music
The Ceremony Music Package includes:
Up to thirty (30) minutes of pre-ceremony music as guests arrive and are seated.
Music for the wedding processional.
Music for the bridal entrance.
Music for the recessional.
Up to three (3) custom song requests arranged for violin and guitar at no additional charge.
Professional sound system and amplification as needed for the ceremony location.
Setup, sound check, and breakdown of all performance equipment.
For packages including amplified ceremony sound, Musician may also provide wireless microphones for the officiant, couple, and/or readers as appropriate.
Cocktail Hour Music
The Cocktail Hour Music Package includes:
Live background music during the cocktail hour.
Repertoire may include Classical, Jazz, Swing, Bossa Nova, Tango, Country, Celtic, Folk, Bluegrass, Pop, and other styles mutually agreed upon.
Professional sound system and amplification as needed for the performance area.
Setup and breakdown of all performance equipment.
Cocktail hour music can include vocals as well as violin and guitar upon request
Unless otherwise specified, cocktail hour music will consist of approximately sixty (60) minutes of continuous live performance.
Audio & Sound Support
Wireless microphones and sound system for the ceremony
Wireless microphones and sound system support for reception announcements and speeches as agreed upon
Event Production
Professional sound technician services throughout the event
Master of Ceremonies (MC) services for key reception announcements
DJ Services
Professional DJ services during the reception
Reception music, announcements, and coordination of major reception events, including:
Grand Entrance
First Dance
Parent Dances
Cake Cutting
Bouquet and/or Garter Toss (if applicable)
Open Dancing
Final Dance

8. Cancellation Policy
Cancellation by Client
The retainer is non-refundable.
If cancellation occurs:
More than 90 days before the event: no additional balance is owed.
Between 30 and 90 days before the event: 50% of the remaining balance becomes due.
Within 30 days of the event: the full contracted amount becomes due.
Cancellation by Musician
If Musician must cancel due to illness, emergency, accident, or circumstances beyond reasonable control, all payments received will be refunded. Musician will also make reasonable efforts to help secure a qualified replacement, but cannot guarantee availability.

9. Force Majeure
Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform due to events beyond reasonable control, including but not limited to natural disasters, severe weather, government restrictions, venue closures, acts of terrorism, epidemics, or other unforeseeable events.
In such cases, both parties will make reasonable efforts to reschedule. If rescheduling is not possible, payments already made, less documented non-recoverable expenses, may be retained by Musician.

10. Limitation of Liability
Musician's liability under this Agreement shall be limited to the total amount paid by the Client under this Agreement.
Musician shall not be responsible for delays or interruptions caused by venue restrictions, power outages, third-party vendors, acts of guests, or circumstances outside of Musician's control.
